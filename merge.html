Merge PDF

Combine two or more PDFs into a single file. Drag to reorder before merging. Everything happens on your device.

    How to merge PDFs with QuickPDF

    1. Drop your PDFs onto the box above, or click it to browse. You can add them a few at a time.
    2. Reorder with the arrow buttons — the merge runs in the order shown.
    3. Click Merge PDFs. The combined file is created in your browser and offered as a download.

    Nothing is uploaded. If you open your browser's developer tools and watch the Network tab, you'll see zero outbound requests carrying your files.

    What merging keeps and loses

    Pages are copied byte-for-byte from the source documents, so text, images, vectors, and annotations all survive. Document-level metadata (title, author, producer) is reset on the merged file. Bookmarks and outlines are not preserved. Encrypted or password-protected PDFs are rejected at the door.

    Size guidance

    Files up to 25 MB are processed instantly. Between 25 and 150 MB you'll see a warning but the tool will still run. Above 150 MB QuickPDF refuses, because browsers on typical hardware run out of memory before the merge completes.

    Merge FAQ

    Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

    Yes. The tool is fully responsive. Large files may hit memory limits sooner on mobile than on desktop, so we recommend merging in smaller batches on phones.

    Does merging reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied as-is from the source PDFs. Only the document wrapper and cross-reference table are rebuilt.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not currently. Handling encrypted files in the browser would require implementing PDF decryption, which we deliberately avoid.
